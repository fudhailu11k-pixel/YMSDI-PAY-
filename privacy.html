1. Who We Are

Youth Mindset and Skills Development Initiative (YMSDI) is an initiative based in Uganda. This Privacy Policy explains how YMSDI Pay collects, uses, stores, protects and shares personal information when users access our website, application and related services.

YMSDI will handle personal information in accordance with applicable Ugandan data protection laws, including the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, and the Data Protection and Privacy Regulations, 2021.

Contact: ymsdi.youth@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the services you use, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: Full name, email address, telephone number and account login information.
  • Identity verification: National identification details and identity document images, where verification is required.
  • Transaction information: Payment amounts, transaction references, dates, fees, wallet addresses and transaction identifiers.
  • Mobile money information: Telephone number and payment references needed to confirm a transaction.
  • Technical information: Device type, browser information, security logs, IP address and login activity, where collected.
  • Support communications: Messages and information you provide when contacting our support team.

We will only collect information that is reasonably necessary for the relevant service or permitted by law.

3. Why We Use Your Information

We may use your personal information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Verify user identity where required.
  • Process and record payments and transactions.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Protect accounts and investigate suspected fraud.
  • Meet applicable legal, financial and regulatory obligations.
  • Maintain platform security and improve our services.
  • Communicate important service updates.

Where consent is required, we will request it before processing the relevant information. Other lawful grounds may apply where permitted by Ugandan law.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell personal information as a business practice.

Where necessary and lawful, we may share relevant information with:

  • Mobile money operators and payment service providers.
  • Crypto or blockchain service providers involved in a transaction.
  • Identity verification providers, where used.
  • Secure hosting, database and email service providers.
  • Professional advisers and competent authorities where legally required.

We aim to limit information sharing to what is necessary for the relevant purpose. Service providers handling information on our behalf must be subject to appropriate confidentiality and data protection safeguards.

5. Where Your Information Is Stored

YMSDI may use cloud hosting, database and other technology providers to operate its services. These providers may store or process information outside Uganda.

Where personal data is transferred or processed outside Uganda, YMSDI will comply with applicable Ugandan requirements concerning international data transfers and appropriate safeguards.

We will identify the relevant service providers and storage arrangements as the platform develops.

6. How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including account management, dispute resolution, security and legal obligations.

Financial and transaction records may need to be retained for a period required by applicable law.

When information is no longer required, we will securely delete it or anonymise it where appropriate, subject to legal retention requirements.

7. How We Protect Your Information

YMSDI will implement appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, which may include:

  • Access controls and restricted administrator permissions.
  • Encryption where appropriate and available.
  • Secure authentication and password protection.
  • Protection of identity documents and sensitive records.
  • Monitoring of suspicious activity and administrator actions.
  • Appropriate backup and recovery procedures.

No electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should protect their login credentials and report suspected unauthorised access promptly.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of information where legally permitted.
  • Object to certain processing or request that processing be restricted where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Raise a complaint about how your information is handled.

Some information may need to be retained to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.

To exercise your rights, contact ymsdi.youth@gmail.com. You may also contact Uganda’s Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) through its official website: https://pdpo.go.ug.

9. Children’s Privacy

YMSDI Pay is intended for adults aged 18 and above where the service involves financial or crypto-related transactions.

We do not knowingly permit children to use services restricted to adults. If we discover that we have collected personal information in breach of applicable age requirements, we will take appropriate steps in accordance with the law.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to maintain sessions, improve security and remember necessary preferences.

Where required, we will provide appropriate notice and obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

Users may be able to manage certain cookies through their browser settings.

11. Blockchain and Cryptocurrency Transactions

If YMSDI Pay supports blockchain transactions, certain transaction information, such as wallet addresses and transaction records, may be publicly visible on the relevant blockchain.

Blockchain records may be difficult or impossible to alter or delete. YMSDI cannot guarantee the removal of information recorded on a public blockchain.

Users should verify wallet addresses, networks, amounts and transaction details before confirming a transfer.

12. Data Breaches and Security Incidents

If a personal data breach occurs, YMSDI will assess the incident and take appropriate steps to contain it, protect affected individuals and notify the relevant authorities or individuals where required by applicable law.

Users should contact us promptly if they suspect their accounts or personal information have been compromised.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology or legal obligations change.

The latest version will be published on our website or application with an updated revision date. Where required, we will notify users of significant changes.
