Learn: stay safe with crypto payments

English | Kiswahili

Short lessons for new members. Swahili text should be checked by a native speaker.

1. What is a stablecoin?1. Stablecoin ni nini?

A stablecoin such as USDT is a digital coin that aims to stay worth about one US dollar. It moves less in price than other crypto, but it still carries risk and is not the same as money in a bank.Stablecoin kama USDT ni sarafu ya kidijitali inayolenga kubaki na thamani ya takriban dola moja ya Marekani. Bei yake hubadilika kidogo kuliko crypto nyingine, lakini bado ina hatari na si sawa na pesa iliyo benki.

2. Always check the network2. Hakikisha mtandao sahihi

If you send USDT on the wrong network (for example Tron instead of BNB Chain), the money can be lost for good. Match the network shown in the app, and send the exact amount.Ukituma USDT kwa mtandao usio sahihi (kwa mfano Tron badala ya BNB Chain), pesa zinaweza kupotea moja kwa moja. Tumia mtandao ulioonyeshwa kwenye programu na utume kiasi kamili.

3. Never share your secret words or password3. Usimpe mtu maneno yako ya siri

Your wallet recovery phrase, PIN and password are yours alone. YMSDI will never ask for them in chat, on a call or by SMS. Anyone who asks is a scammer.Maneno ya siri ya pochi yako (recovery phrase), PIN na nenosiri ni yako peke yako. YMSDI hatakuomba kamwe kwa ujumbe, simu au SMS. Anayeomba ni tapeli.

4. Spot the scams4. Tambua ulaghai

Be careful with promises of guaranteed profit, pressure to act fast, strangers offering help, and fake support accounts. If it sounds too good to be true, it is.Jihadhari na ahadi za faida ya uhakika, shinikizo la kufanya haraka, watu wasiokujua wanaotoa msaada, na akaunti bandia za msaada. Kinachosikika kizuri kupita kiasi mara nyingi ni ulaghai.

5. Check before you pay5. Kagua kabla ya kulipa

Re-read the phone, meter or account number, the amount and the fee. Crypto payments are hard to reverse, so a typo can cost you.Soma tena namba ya simu, mita au akaunti, kiasi na ada. Malipo ya crypto ni vigumu kurudisha, kwa hiyo kosa dogo linaweza kukugharimu.

6. Keep your receipts and ask for help6. Hifadhi risiti na omba msaada

Save your receipt numbers. If something goes wrong, contact support straight away with the receipt number.Hifadhi namba za risiti zako. Ukiona tatizo, wasiliana na msaada mara moja ukiwa na namba ya risiti.
